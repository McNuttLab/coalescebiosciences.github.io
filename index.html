Coalesce Biosciences is a specialized Contract Research Organization (CRO) advancing preclinical testing for highly hazardous biological and chemical threat agents. We operate at the leading edge of biodefense and industrial safety. Coalesce is uniquely positioned with the regulatory clearance and specialized infrastructure required to handle Schedule I and II chemical agents and high-potency biological toxins. Our facilities are designed for the secure management of hazardous substances that many other labs simply cannot accommodate.
